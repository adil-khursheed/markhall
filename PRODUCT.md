# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Class 9–10 students preparing at home for board exams across CBSE, ICSE/CISCE, state boards, IB/IGCSE, and other boards. Their job is to practise chapters, take timed tests, and cover the syllabus with feedback that reflects how exam answers are marked.

## Product Purpose

An exam-prep platform combining three things in one place: AI-assisted practice with answer feedback, mock tests with scoring and performance analytics, and a structured content library of notes, syllabus material, and question banks. Markhall uses one board-neutral core so students can choose their board and class without the product speaking only to one examination system. Success means measurable improvement in students' board exam scores.

## Operating Context

Students use it for self-directed revision outside school, typically alone at home, working toward Class 9–10 board exams. Teachers and parents are not confirmed as primary users. The current build includes a working seating-plan prototype with board and class selection and sample content; the content is not yet board-specific.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Initial scope is Class 9–10 across CBSE, ICSE/CISCE, state boards, IB/IGCSE, and other boards.
- Board and class selection exists and persists locally, but the sample question bank is shared across boards. Do not imply board-specific syllabi, official past papers, or examined content.
- The product is expected to combine AI practice and feedback, mock tests and analytics, and a content library.
- No licensed content, official question bank, notes, or past-paper data exists yet; content must be authored or generated rather than presented as existing.
- Built on Payload CMS 3 with a Next.js 16 frontend and a Postgres database.
- Markhall is not affiliated with any board or examination authority.
- Open decision: board-specific content coverage, content licensing, pricing, and account roles beyond the default users collection are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No published question banks, notes, past-paper data, testimonials, customers, pricing, or performance claims exist. Future work must not fabricate them. The current questions and marks are labelled sample content.

## Product Principles

- Optimise for exam score improvement above engagement metrics.
- Keep practice and feedback tied to the student's selected board and class, and label the limits of sample content honestly.
- Build the three pillars as one coherent student experience, not three disconnected tools.
- Keep the core experience board-neutral first; add board-specific content only when it can be authored and verified.
- Never invent content, affiliations, or evidence; author it explicitly or leave it marked undecided.
